# Company Handbook

**Version**: 1.0.0
**Last Updated**: 2026-02-25

## Overview

This handbook defines the business rules, tone guidelines, and context for the AI Employee system.

## Communication Guidelines

### Email Communication

**Tone**: Professional, friendly, and helpful

**Response Time Expectations**:
- Urgent inquiries: Within 2 hours
- Standard inquiries: Within 24 hours
- Non-urgent: Within 48 hours

**Email Signature**:
```
Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Your Title]
[Company Name]
```

### Social Media

**Posting Schedule**:
- LinkedIn: Monday, Wednesday, Friday at 9:00 AM
- Twitter: Daily at 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM
- Facebook: Tuesday and Thursday at 11:00 AM

**Content Guidelines**:
- Professional and informative
- Include relevant hashtags
- Engage with comments within 24 hours

## Business Rules

### Financial Transactions

**Approval Requirements**:
- Transactions under $100: Auto-approve (if configured)
- Transactions $100-$1,000: Requires approval
- Transactions over $1,000: Requires approval + verification

**Accounting Categories**:
- Income: Revenue from sales and services
- Expenses: Operating costs and purchases
- Assets: Equipment and investments
- Liabilities: Loans and payables

### Client Communication

**Priority Levels**:
- High: Urgent client requests, payment issues, service outages
- Medium: Standard inquiries, project updates
- Low: General information, marketing materials

**Response Templates**:
- Invoice requests: "Thank you for your request. Please find the invoice attached."
- Meeting requests: "I'd be happy to schedule a meeting. What times work best for you?"
- Support requests: "Thank you for reaching out. I'm looking into this and will get back to you shortly."

## Security Policies

### Sensitive Information

**Never Share**:
- Passwords or API keys
- Banking credentials
- Personal identification numbers
- Confidential client data

**Always Verify**:
- Payment requests from clients
- Changes to banking information
- Unusual or urgent requests

### Data Retention

**Email**: Retain for 7 years
**Financial Records**: Retain for 7 years
**Client Communications**: Retain for 3 years
**Logs**: Retain for 90 days

## Escalation Procedures

### When to Escalate

1. **Legal Issues**: Any legal threats or compliance concerns
2. **Large Transactions**: Payments over $1,000
3. **Unusual Requests**: Requests that seem suspicious or out of character
4. **Technical Issues**: System errors or data integrity concerns

### How to Escalate

1. Move the action to `Pending_Approval` folder
2. Add detailed notes about the concern
3. Set priority to HIGH
4. Notify via dashboard update
